\begin{center}
\textbf{\large Note on Prior Dissemination}
\end{center}
\bigskip

The study reported in this thesis has also been disseminated as a preprint,
\emph{Generative Augmentation of Raman Spectra for Glioma Classification}
\cite{iusan2026raman}, prepared for submission to a peer-reviewed journal. The article
presents the same dataset, methodology and experimental results in condensed form; the
present thesis is the fuller account, and additionally includes the theoretical background
of Chapter~\ref{ch:theory}, the complete cross-validation protocol of
Appendix~\ref{app:cv_schema}, and the extended per-experiment results of
Appendix~\ref{app:extended_results}, which the article omits for reasons of length.

\bigskip

The contributions to that article were as follows. The candidate designed and implemented the
preprocessing pipeline, the generative model and the evaluation framework, conducted all
experiments, and wrote the manuscript. Iulian Vasile contributed to methodological decisions
and prepared the discussion section and its figures. Daria Voiculescu contributed editing and
literature review. Ion Petre and Bogdan Oancea provided supervision and manuscript revision.
Andrei Păun and Mihaela Păun contributed manuscript review.
