We report complete results of the downstream classifiers for all of the experiments in this project. We select five aspects, each varying by one factor: (1) the CVAE's $D_{KL}$ loss weight $\beta$, (2) the nominal latent dimension $d$, (3) the reconstruction-loss weighting $\gamma$, (4) the synthetic/real ratio used by TSR/TR, and (5) the strong-signal mask's max-intensity threshold \texttt{thr}. We compare all experiments with the shared anchor \texttt{exp\_608} (configuration: $\beta=0.5$, $d=32$, $\gamma=1$, synthetic/real ratio = 1, \texttt{thr}=0.6). 

We report the bag-level accuracy for each training regimen (TR/TR: real only; TS/TR: synthetic only; TSR/TR: synthetic+real) under the naïve MIL approach (one majority-vote decision per bag, i.e. per biopsy slide), and the bootstrap difference $\Delta$ relative to the TR/TR baseline with its 95\% bootstrap confidence interval; (*) marks a 95\% CI excluding zero, and four additional bag-level metrics: balanced accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and AUC.

The bootstrap $\Delta$/CI/significance columns are computed for raw accuracy. We report the balanced accuracy as well and we mention that given the fairly balanced nature of this dataset for the binary IDH classification task, the two metrics are close.
The CbR row reports the bag-level accuracy and 95\% CI for it, and it does not represent a difference vs.\ TR/TR.

\subsection*{MIL tier results}\label{app:mil-results}

MIL tier experiments were performed for the most relevant configurations. Below are all the results with the same metrics as the downstream classifiers: accuracy, balanced accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, and AUC. We note that mi-SVM and Markov MIL are independent of $\beta$, and the others are built on top of the CVAE.
% Full MIL tier results (methodology, spatial validation, shuffled-label controls) are reported in full in the project's own MIL findings reference. The five bag-level metrics below are reproduced here for the experiments in this family that have them, using the same convention as the downstream-classifier tables above: balanced accuracy and sensitivity/specificity as per-class recall (class 1 / class 0), AUC from the continuous bag-level diagnostic-class score. For mi-SVM/attention/CbR this score is the pooled, renormalized $(K{+}1)$-way posterior's diagnostic column; for Bayesian MIL's blind classification (\texttt{score\_bayes\_mil\_blind}) it is a softmax of the duplicate-bag-averaged posterior-predictive log-likelihood, since that path returns log-likelihoods, not a normalized probability. Bayesian MIL's Gibbs-sampled posterior is PCA-based and does not depend on $\beta$, which is why mi-SVM and Bayesian MIL are identical across \texttt{exp\_607}/\texttt{608}/\texttt{609} and again across \texttt{exp\_2.607}/\texttt{2.608}.
